Composite cells Several columns drawn in the one cell

A composite cell draws several columns at once. The first is the headline - what the cell reads as and sorts by - the rest are on hover, and the ▾ in the header sorts the grid by any of them. Every member is still exported to CSV and offered in the filter builder. The example cells below are drawn by the grid’s own renderers from made up data - no real variant, sample or lab appears on this page.
