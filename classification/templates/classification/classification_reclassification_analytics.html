Germline Reclassification Analytics

{% page_help_embedded title="Germline Reclassification Analytics" %}

How germline clinical significance has moved over time, mined from every published version of every classification record. Each record contributes an initial classification, then one event per time a curator revisited it - a re-evaluation where the curation dates advanced and the call held, a reclassification where the call moved.

Everything on this page is germline. Somatic records are curated on their own clinical significance axis, so they sit outside these numbers entirely.

An external lab's event dates are the date the record synced in, not the date that lab curated it, so narrow the Labs filter to internal labs to read the time based charts as curation dates.

Maintenance by Lab

What each lab holds, how much of it the lab has revisited, and how much of it has changed. Records held is every record with a timeline by the end of the window, so a lab that joined part way through is measured on the records it actually had. The baseline is what a lab of that size would reclassify at the median per record rate across labs with any activity, so a positive residual means the lab reanalyses more than its size predicts.
