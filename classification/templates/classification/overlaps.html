{% page_help_embedded title="Overlaps and Discordances" %}

An overlap is defined as an allele where two or more different labs provide records within the same testing context.
Testing contexts include Germline, Non-Cancer Somatic, Haematology, Solid Tumour.
When an overlap is discordant, labs can triage their submissions from this page.

: overlaps that are discordant where your lab(s) records have not yet been triaged.
: all relevant labs have triaged but not come to a concordance.
: if during a previous triage you agreed to change a value, the changes will be listed here.
: your lab(s) have triaged, but another lab still needs to triage.
: shows all overlaps where every involved lab has provided VUS (or VUS-A,B,C). No action is required here.
: shows overlaps your lab(s) contribute towards that were once actively discordant.
: shows every instance where you and another lab contribute to the same overlap, regardless of agreement/disagreement.
: only relevant for somatic labs, shows all overlaps where two or more labs have provided somatic clinical significance.
