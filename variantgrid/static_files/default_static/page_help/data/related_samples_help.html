Calculated using Somalier (Paper). See Relatedness help

Pairs are kept when they clear the shared HET, shared HOM ALT and relatedness minimums shown above the table on the sample page (they are set per deployment in settings.SOMALIER["relatedness"]).

How the genotypes are decided

VariantGrid imports every call a VCF makes, however low its depth or quality - we never drop one. So relatedness is QC'd by Somalier instead: we hand it the imported allele depths and it re-genotypes from those, counting only sites with a read depth of at least 7 and, for a HET, an allele balance between 0.3 and 0.7. Expect the HET/HOM counts Somalier reports to sit below the sample extract counts at the bottom of this tab - that difference is the QC doing its job.

A VCF imported without depth information (no allele depth field, or no read depth / allele frequency to derive a reference depth from) has nothing to QC with, so those samples are related on their genotypes exactly as called.

Samples from different VCFs

A jointly called VCF records a 0/0 for a site a sample was called reference at, so a site missing from that sample means "unknown". A VCF that never writes 0/0 - merged single-sample calls, a benchmark VCF, anything derived from a gVCF - means a missing site is reference, and Somalier is run with --unknown to say so. The same is true of the nightly all-vs-all run, where samples come from many different VCFs.

Relatedness under --unknown is understated compared to a jointly called VCF: a trio measured 0.51 jointly called and 0.33 with --unknown. Duplicates still come out near 1.0, but a parent/child pair may sit in the 0.3 - 0.5 band rather than at 0.5.

Interpreting relatedness

  • 1.00 = Identical twins/Clones/Duplicate sample
  • 0.50 = Parent/Child/Full Sibling/Parent’s Identical twin/Identical twin’s child
  • 0.35 = 3/4 sibling (e.g. same mother, fathers are siblings)
  • 0.25 = Grandparent/Grandchild/Half-sibling/Uncle/Aunt/Niece/Nephew/Double-1st Cousin
  • <0.20 = Distant relative (cousins) / Not related
