A cohort is a set of samples analysed together - in a cohort node, as Duo/Trio/Quad members, or for gene counts and hotspots. Every uploaded VCF is already a cohort of its samples; make your own to pick samples from one or more files.

  • Samples from one VCF make a sub cohort: ready instantly, and it keeps the caller's genotype at every site - homozygous reference (0/0) is distinct from no call (./.).
  • Samples from several VCFs are merged into new genotype data by a background database job. A sample has no record at a site its own VCF didn't call, so it is stored as no call there, matched by a zygosity filter's "none" option and never by REF. The cohort can't tell a reference call from no coverage.
  • For a family, joint call them into one VCF from the BAMs and upload that. A de novo or recessive call relies on each parent being REF at the site, which only a joint call records. In a merged cohort, a parent with no coverage there looks the same as one who is REF, so de novo results can't be confirmed. A case series without inheritance filters is less affected. A large merged cohort is one long database job. See Multi-sample VCFs for why merging the VCF files alone is not enough.
