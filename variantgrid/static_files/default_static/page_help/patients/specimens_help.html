A specimen is biological material collected from a patient - one tissue at one timepoint, such as a tumour block or a blood draw. The nucleic acid taken off it is an extraction.

Specimens normally arrive by patient record import. The CSV names the specimen beside the sample it came from, so one import creates the patient, the specimen and its extraction, and links the sample to it - see import patient records details for the columns.

A specimen can also be added by hand on a patient's Specimens tab. A later import matches on the patient and the reference, so it fills in what the CSV knows rather than creating a second specimen.
