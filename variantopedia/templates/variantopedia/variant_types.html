Why these thresholds
Stored as symbolic from {{ symbolic_alt_size }}
A deletion, duplication or inversion of {{ symbolic_alt_size }} or more is stored as a symbolic <DEL>/<DUP>/<INV> with a length (SVLEN) rather than its full sequence - {{ symbolic_alt_size }} is the usual definition of a structural variant. However it arrives (VCF, HGVS, symbolic or explicit), a variant has one stored form, and analyses filter symbolic variants under the "Structural" variant type rather than "Indel". Insertions that don't duplicate the sequence beside them have no symbolic form here and keep their sequence at any size.
Annotated as a short variant below {{ structural_variant_min_size }}
Annotation uses a separate, larger threshold. A symbolic variant under {{ structural_variant_min_size }} is still annotated by the short variant pipeline, given to VEP as its full sequence. gnomAD's short variant data has exact allele frequencies for indels into the hundreds of bases, while the structural variant pipeline can only match an overlapping gnomAD-SV call. From {{ structural_variant_min_size }} a variant is annotated as a structural variant: gene overlaps, gnomAD-SV {% if annotsv_enabled %}and AnnotSV{% endif %}, without the per-base scores (eg SpliceAI, CADD, dbNSFP) that only exist for short variants.
{% if vep_sv_max_size %}
Not annotated by VEP over {{ vep_sv_max_size }}
VEP won't annotate a structural variant longer than {{ vep_sv_max_size }}, so these are not sent to it, and show "Not annotated by VEP" on the variant page. Their overlapping genes are still worked out here from the transcripts, so gene filters and gene lists still find them, but they have no consequence, transcript annotation, HGVS or gnomAD-SV frequency.
{% endif %}
