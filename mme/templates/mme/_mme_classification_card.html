MatchMaker Exchange is a federated rare-disease network. Labs query each other for patients who share a candidate gene and phenotype, so an unsolved case can find a second one like it.

What is shared: the candidate gene and variant coordinates, the curated condition, HPO phenotype terms, and your lab's contact details. No patient identifiers, no names, no dates of birth — a record is identified only by an opaque id.

VUS and above only. The MME protocol has no field for clinical significance, so every submission reads as "we hold a case where this gene is a candidate". A Benign call sent through it would arrive as corroboration of someone else's lead. Publish negatives to ClinVar, which carries clinical significance as a first-class field and already receives these same records.

Three layers of opt-in, all of which must hold: this deployment participates, your lab has enabled MatchMaker Exchange, and this record is shared at "3rd Party Databases" level, not withdrawn, and VUS or above.

A match is an introduction, not an answer: it results in a person-to-person email between curators. Both your lab's contact address and you are notified.
